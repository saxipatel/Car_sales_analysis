# DAX Measures Documentation

This document lists the DAX measures used in the Car Sales Analysis Power BI dashboard.

## 1. Sales Performance

| Measure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| YTD Total Sales | Calculates year-to-date sales revenue |
| Sales Difference | Compares YTD sales with the previous year |
| YoY Sales Growth | Measures year-over-year sales growth percentage |
| MTD KPI | Displays month-to-date sales |
| Total Sales | Displays transaction-level sales values |

## 2. Average Selling Price

| Measure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| YTD Avg Price | Calculates the average vehicle selling price year to date |
| Avg Price Difference | Compares average prices against the previous year |
| YoY Avg Price Growth | Measures the percentage change in average selling price |
| MTD Avg Price KPI | Displays month-to-date average selling price |

## 3. Vehicle Sales Volume

| Measure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| YTD Cars Sold | Calculates the number of vehicles sold year to date |
| Cars Sold Difference | Compares vehicle sales volume with the previous year |
| YoY Cars Sold Growth | Measures year-over-year growth in vehicles sold |
| MTD Cars Sold KPI | Displays month-to-date vehicle sales |

## 4. Supporting Measures

| Measure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Max Point | Highlights the highest point in the weekly sales trend |
